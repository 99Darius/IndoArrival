Mistake on your All Indonesia card? How to update it

Checked 5 November 2026 by Darius Cheung. Independent guide, not a government website.

Short answer

The site says "you can update the submitted information prior to the trip", and goes further: "You must immediately update your information if there is a change." So a changed flight or hotel, or a wrong detail, should be fixed through the update option before you travel. If that isn't possible, a new submission is the fallback, and you should use the latest QR code.

What the site says

Three lines in the form's own text set this out:

  • "If need be, you can update the submitted information prior to the trip."
  • "You must immediately update your information if there is a change."
  • "Are you sure you want to update the application data?" (the confirmation you'll see)

That second line matters more than most guides let on. It isn't optional tidying. If your flight changes, the card should say so.

How to update

  1. Go to allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id and open the option to retrieve your arrival card.
  2. Enter your details as you submitted them. If you registered an account, your history is there as well.
  3. Open the submission and choose to change the data.
  4. Check the summary and confirm. Then save the QR code again, in case a new one is issued.

We read this flow from the site's code and text, and haven't run it, because we don't submit test data to government forms. The screens may differ a little.

What if the fix is to your passport details?

The site tells you: "Make sure your information matches the one on your passport. Please feel free to edit any wrong details." That applies during filing, when it's easy. After submission, we can't say from the outside which fields stay editable. If the update screen won't let you change a passport detail, filing a new card is the fallback. Third-party guides say to keep the latest QR code, and one suggests changes are possible until the code has been scanned at immigration.

Common situations

What changedWhat to do
Flight number or timeUpdate the card.
Hotel or addressUpdate the card.
Arrival date, still within 72 hoursUpdate the card.
Arrival date moved days laterYou may need to wait until the new date is within the 72-hour window, then update or file again.
Wrong email, so no QR codeUse retrieve to get the code, then correct the address. See the QR code guide.
New passport since you filedFile a new card with the new passport.
Goods or health answers changedUpdate the declaration. These answers need to be true on the day you arrive.

Check before you press Submit

The form says: "Please review the answers you have provided for each section below before submission." It's worth doing. Declaration answers carry real consequences: the site warns that items exceeding allowances may be confiscated and destroyed by officers, and that false information can break the rules.

Questions people ask

Can I change my All Indonesia card after submitting?

Yes, the site says you can update it before the trip, and that you must if something changes.

Should I file a second card if I made a mistake?

Try updating first. If the screen won't allow the change, file a new one, and show the latest QR code.

What if my flight is delayed after I've left for the airport?

If you can get online, update it. If you can't, explain to the officer on arrival. We can't speak for how they'll treat it.
