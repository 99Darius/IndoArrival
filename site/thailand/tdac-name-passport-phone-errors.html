TDAC rejects my name, passport or phone: the exact rules

Checked 5 November 2026 by Darius Cheung. Independent guide, not a government website.

Short answer

TDAC fields accept narrow sets of characters. First and middle names take English letters and spaces only, and family names add one exception: a lone dash. So an apostrophe or hyphen fails. Passport numbers take letters and digits with no spaces, and phone numbers take digits only. Type names the way the machine-readable lines at the bottom of your passport print them, without the punctuation.

If your name has an apostrophe, hyphen or accent

Look at the two machine-readable lines at the bottom of your passport's photo page. International passport standards remove apostrophes, replace hyphens with a filler, and convert accents into plain letters, so those lines already show a form of your name the system can handle. For example:

  • O'BRIEN prints as OBRIEN, so type OBRIEN.
  • SMITH-JONES prints as SMITH<JONES, so type SMITH JONES.
  • An accented MÜLLER may print as MUELLER or MULLER. Use what your passport's lines show.

This is our reading of the standard and the form's rules. It isn't official advice from Thai immigration, who have the last word at the border.

If you have no family name

Some passports show only one name. The family name pattern allows exactly one dash and nothing else, which suggests - is the intended entry. The form requires this box, so an empty one fails. First name has no such exception.

Things that catch people out

  • Letter O and number 0. The most common passport typo. The form can't spot it, because both are valid characters.
  • Pasted spaces. Copying from a booking email can bring a space with it, which fails on the passport number.
  • You won't see the error until you leave the field. The form checks a value when you click away from it, so it can look fine while you're typing.
  • You can't update a name later. The site says full name can't be updated after submission, so a typo there means a new submission.

Uploads have rules too: a passport image must be JPG, JPEG, PNG or BMP up to 5MB, and a vaccination certificate must be JPG, JPEG, PNG or PDF up to 5MB with a file name under 250 characters.

Questions people ask

Does the TDAC have to match my passport exactly?

The form says full name in passport. Since it can't accept punctuation, use the plain-letter form shown in your passport's machine-readable lines.

Do I type my phone's country code twice?

No. The code goes in its own box and the number field takes digits only. Leave out the plus sign.

Can I write my hotel in Thai?

No. The address field rejects Thai script. See the accommodation guide for what to type.
