Arrival card form not working? Start here

Checked 5 November 2026 by Darius Cheung. Independent guide, not a government website.

Short answer

Most arrival card failures come down to one of five things: the wrong website, a date outside the three-day window, a character the field rejects, a session that timed out, or an email that went to spam. Check them in that order. If the form still fails, you can usually finish at the airport: Thailand has kiosks at five airports, and its embassy says the card can be completed until you reach immigration.

Check these five things, in this order

  1. Are you on the government's site? Look at the address bar. Thailand's card is on tdac.immigration.go.th and Indonesia's is on allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id. If a site is asking for a card payment for the arrival card itself, you are probably on a copy. See the guides for Thailand and Indonesia.
  2. Is your arrival date in the window? Both countries work to about 72 hours before you land. On Thailand's form, a date further out simply can't be picked. See the TDAC date guide.
  3. Did a field reject what you typed? Forms like these check strict patterns. On the TDAC, an apostrophe in a surname or Thai script in a hotel address will fail. See the field rules.
  4. Did the session expire? If you left the page open while hunting for your hotel's address, you may have to start again. Have the details ready first.
  5. Did it work after all? A missing email doesn't always mean a failed submission. Thailand's confirmation and Indonesia's QR code each have a recovery route.

Who actually runs these forms

Each form belongs to that country's immigration authority, and only they can fix a fault on their site or accept your card. No app, agent or browser extension can get a form accepted when the government's system is down. A paid "urgent" service can't make it faster either, because free submissions go through the same system.

So when a site is truly down, your job is to keep your details handy and try again later, then fall back on the airport. Indonesia's own terms say the government "does not guarantee uninterrupted access to the application", which is a fair warning not to leave it until you're in the taxi to the airport.

Leave yourself a margin

Do it the day before you fly rather than in the departure queue. Have your passport, flight number and hotel address open before you start, and note that the address needs to be in English letters. If several people are travelling, allow time for each of them, because both forms ask for every traveller's details, including babies.

Questions people ask

Will I be refused entry if the form fails?

We can't promise anything for your trip, and officers decide at the border. Thailand's embassy says the TDAC can be completed until you reach immigration, and lists kiosks at BKK, DMK, HKT, CNX and HDY. For Indonesia, carry your details and ask staff on arrival if you truly couldn't submit.

Does the arrival card replace a visa?

No. Both countries say so. Whether you need a visa depends on your nationality and your trip, and is a separate check.
