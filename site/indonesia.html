All Indonesia Arrival Card guide

Checked 5 November 2026 by Darius Cheung. Independent guide, not a government website.

Short answer

The All Indonesia Arrival Card is a free online form that combines immigration, customs and health declarations. Everyone arriving needs their own, children and infants included. File it at allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id within 72 hours before you land. You get a QR code by email, which you show with your passport. It isn't a visa, and you don't need an account: there's a guest option.

What it replaced

It folds together what used to be separate: the immigration arrival card, the SATUSEHAT health declaration and the e-CD customs declaration. If an old guide tells you to do those individually, it's out of date.

Who needs one

All international travellers arriving in Indonesia, including tourists and business travellers. Indonesian citizens returning home do too, using a separate form on the same site. The site checks this: if your passport doesn't match the form, you may see "The passport submitted does not indicate Indonesian citizenship. Please ensure you are using the correct application form for foreign visitor."

What to have ready

  • Passport: number, nationality, issue and expiry dates, name, birth date. An expired one stops you: "This passport has expired. Please use a passport that is still valid to continue."
  • Contact: an email you can open abroad, because the QR code goes there, and a phone number. The form reminds you: "Please make sure your email address is correct".
  • Trip: arrival and departure dates, visa or stay permit number if you have one, flight or ship details and port of entry.
  • Stay: hotel name and address.
  • Declarations: recent countries visited, symptoms, goods to declare (including cash, animals and certain foods) and number of bags. Phones may need their IMEI registered.

How the form runs

  1. Go to the official site, switch to English if needed, and choose Foreign Visitor.
  2. Choose to submit as a guest, or log in. The guest route needs no account.
  3. Enter passport, trip, transport and address details, then the health, customs and quarantine questions.
  4. Review the summary. The site asks you to confirm before you submit.
  5. Save the QR code on screen and check for the email.

When something goes wrong

  • The form won't submit or shows an error
  • The QR code never arrived, or you lost it
  • You made a mistake, or your plans changed
  • You're travelling as a family or group
  • You may have paid a copy of the form

Other requirements still apply

The card doesn't replace a visa. Your passport generally needs six months' validity, and you may be asked for an onward ticket. Those are separate from the form, and the guide linked below lists them. Check the rules for your nationality before you fly.

Questions people ask

Is the All Indonesia card free?

Yes. Australia's Smartraveller says it should be free and warns about fake sites charging a fee. A visa on arrival, if you need one, is a separate paid item.

Do I need an account?

No. The site offers "Submission of Arrival Card as Guest". An account lets you save profiles and see your history from the last 90 days.

Is there an app?

Yes, an official All Indonesia app also exists, according to third-party guides. We only cover the website here, since that's where our extension works.
