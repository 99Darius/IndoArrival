Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026. Applies to the ArrivalFiller browser extension (called IndoArrival before version 1.1), version 1.0 and later, and to this website.

The short version

Everything the ArrivalFiller extension saves stays in your own browser. We do not run a server for it, we do not receive your data, and we do not sell or share it. This website is separate: it counts visits with Google Analytics, as explained below.

Who we are

ArrivalFiller is made by Darius Cheung, an individual developer. Contact: darius.cheung@gmail.com.

What the extension saves, and where

Only on the official arrival card websites it supports, and only to fill those forms for you, the extension saves in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local). Those websites are the All Indonesia card (allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id) and Thailand's Digital Arrival Card (tdac.immigration.go.th).

  • The names you give travellers (for example "Alex").
  • The answers each traveller entered on the arrival card, which can include full name, date of birth, passport number and expiry, nationality, country of birth, gender, phone number, email, visa or stay-permit number, occupation, country and city of residence, purpose of travel, flights, port of entry and accommodation. Each country keeps its own travellers and answers.
  • That you accepted the first-run notice, and when.
  • Which country you last picked.
  • How many arrival cards it has filled and whether it has asked you for a review, so it knows when to ask again. Every few cards it shows a "Leave a review" link to the Chrome Web Store, which you can turn off with "Never ask again".

On Indonesia's card it remembers your usual declaration answers (such as health questions and goods to declare) so you can check them next time. On Thailand's card it remembers your health step answers (countries visited, yellow fever vaccination and its date, and symptoms) in the same way, and the email you give on the last page, but never a certificate file. It can open that page's terms and conditions for you, and you read them and press Agree yourself. You review every answer and press Submit yourself. It does not read other websites, your browsing history, or your Google account.

If you taught one country's form and are now teaching the other, ArrivalFiller offers common details saved for travellers in this browser (passport number, date of birth and phone number, plus gender going from Indonesia to Thailand and name going from Thailand to Indonesia). It shows you whose they are and the values first, and uses them only if you choose to. This happens inside your browser. This data never leaves your device through ArrivalFiller. It is not sent to the developer or any third party. Browser storage is not encrypted by the extension; anyone who can use your browser profile can see it, as with saved passwords and autofill.

Advertising

The extension shows one advertisement at a time. theAsianparent, a parenting app, appears for every country. On Indonesia's card it takes turns with Rumah123, a property website, and a small counter saved in your browser remembers whose turn is next. The ads are plain links: no tracking code, no cookies and no data about you are sent to either advertiser by ArrivalFiller. If you click one, your browser opens rumah123.com, Apple's App Store or Google Play; the Rumah123 and Google Play links carry a tag saying the visit came from this extension. From then on, that site's own privacy policy applies. Neither advertiser has access to your ArrivalFiller data, and neither is responsible for the extension.

Update emails (website only)

This website has an optional form to get emails about ArrivalFiller, such as when it has changed to keep up with the arrival card or has new features. If you sign up, we store the email address you type and the date, encrypted, and use it only to send those emails. It is never sold or shared. Email darius.cheung@gmail.com to be removed. The extension itself never collects your email or sends anything anywhere.

Analytics

The extension: none. It has no analytics, telemetry or crash reporting, and it never sends your answers or passport details to us or to Google.

This website: we use Google Analytics to learn how many people read our guides, which pages they read, how they found us, and whether they go on to the Chrome Web Store. Google receives the page address, your browser and device type, your approximate location and your IP address, and it sets cookies to tell visits apart. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland it runs without analytics cookies. We turn off advertising features and Google signals. Nothing you type into a government form or the extension is involved. You can opt out with a browser setting or Google's opt-out add-on, and Google explains its handling in its privacy policy. Our analytics account is held by Moat Venture Pte Ltd, which helps run this website's infrastructure.

Deleting your data

  • Delete one traveller with the × next to their name.
  • Delete everything with Erase all data in the extension.
  • Uninstalling the extension also deletes everything it stored.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

ArrivalFiller complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the form-filling feature, is not transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising, credit decisions or any other purpose.

Not an official service

ArrivalFiller is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated for the Government of Indonesia, its Directorate General of Immigration, the Government of Thailand or the Immigration Bureau of Thailand. You review every answer and press Submit yourself, and you remain responsible for what you declare.
