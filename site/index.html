What it does

🛂
Fills every page

Passport, name, birth date, nationality, contact details, flights and accommodation. Your usual declaration and health answers too, for you to check are still true.

👨‍👩‍👧
Several travellers

Keep answers for yourself, a partner, the kids. Switch in one click.

✨
Doesn't ask twice

Filled one country's card? On the next it offers your passport number, date of birth and other common details from your saved records. You choose whether to use them.

📅
Smart trip dates

Learns your pattern, like "arrive tomorrow, leave 14 days later", instead of fixed dates.

🔒
Stays in your browser

The extension has no account, no server and no analytics. Your answers are never sent to us.

✋
You stay in control

It never presses Next, Continue or Submit. You check every answer and submit yourself.

Using it

  1. Install ArrivalFiller, pick the country and name a traveller (for example yourself).
  2. Open the arrival card (allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id or tdac.immigration.go.th) and fill it once as usual.
  3. Press Done. Those answers are saved for that traveller.
  4. Next trip: pick the traveller, press Fill on each page, check, then move on and press Submit yourself.

Questions

What does ArrivalFiller do?

It's a free Chrome extension for the official arrival cards of Indonesia and Thailand. Fill a card once as usual and it remembers that traveller's answers. Next trip, press Fill on each page, check everything, and submit yourself.

Is it an official government service?

No. It's an independent tool, not affiliated with either government. It fills the official form on the official website, and you remain responsible for what you declare.

Does it submit the card for me?

No. It never presses Next, Continue, Agree or Submit. You check each answer and press those yourself.

Where do my passport details go?

Nowhere. The extension stores them only in your browser's extension storage. It has no account, no server and no analytics, and uninstalling it deletes everything.

Can it fix an error on the government's site?

No. If the site is down or rejects a request, only the government can fix that. It does save you retyping when you have to start again.

Does it cost anything?

No, and the arrival cards themselves are free too. Be careful of websites that charge a fee to submit them.
