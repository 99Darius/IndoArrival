What it does

🛂
Fills every page

Passport, name, birth date, nationality, contact details, flight, port of entry, accommodation and your usual declaration answers.

👨‍👩‍👧
Several travellers

Keep answers for yourself, a partner, the kids. Switch in one click.

📅
Smart trip dates

Learns your pattern, like "arrive tomorrow, leave 14 days later", instead of fixed dates.

🔒
Stays in your browser

No account, no server, no analytics. Nothing is sent to us.

✋
You stay in control

It never presses Next or Submit. You check every answer and submit yourself.

Using it

  1. Install IndoArrival and name a traveller (for example yourself).
  2. Open the arrival card at allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id and fill it once as usual.
  3. Press Done in the panel. Those answers are saved for that traveller.
  4. Next trip: pick the traveller, press Fill on each page, check, then press Next and Submit yourself.
