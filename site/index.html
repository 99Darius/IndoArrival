A free browser extension for the official arrival cards of Indonesia and Thailand. Fill a card once as usual and ArrivalFiller remembers each traveller's answers. Next trip, press Fill, check, and submit. It used to be called IndoArrival.

What it does

🛂
Fills every page

Passport, name, birth date, nationality, contact details, flights and accommodation. Your usual declaration and health answers too, for you to check are still true.

👨‍👩‍👧
Several travellers

Keep answers for yourself, a partner, the kids. Switch in one click.

✨
Doesn't ask twice

Filled one country's card? On the next it offers your passport number, date of birth and other common details from your saved records. You choose whether to use them.

📅
Smart trip dates

Learns your pattern, like "arrive tomorrow, leave 14 days later", instead of fixed dates.

🔒
Stays in your browser

No account, no server, no analytics. Nothing is sent to us.

✋
You stay in control

It never presses Next, Continue or Submit. You check every answer and submit yourself.

Using it

  1. Install ArrivalFiller, pick the country and name a traveller (for example yourself).
  2. Open the arrival card (allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id or tdac.immigration.go.th) and fill it once as usual.
  3. Press Done. Those answers are saved for that traveller.
  4. Next trip: pick the traveller, press Fill on each page, check, then move on and press Submit yourself.
